# NTM Training Mode

(This doesn't exist yet, I'm just writing some ideas for future reference)


## Motivation

I want to improve the accuracy of my approach shots in Neo Turf Masters.

Currently my options are:
- Play normal rounds (either 1p or 2p).
- Use uniBIOS to practice with hole select and controllable wind (usually for setups).
- Use uniBIOS to manually place the ball and try shots from various distances (also with controllable wind).

These are viable options, but not exactly perfect.

I'd benefit more from some kind of dedicated "training mode".

## Ideas

- I want the ability to start and stop a training session.

- The purpose of a training session is to be presented with various shot conditions

- During the session, precise measurements should be taken, such as:
  - Distance from pin.
  - Time taken per shot.
  - 

- The session should swap between various conditions

A desirable outcome would be the ability to see which shots/distances/conditions I struggle with the most.
